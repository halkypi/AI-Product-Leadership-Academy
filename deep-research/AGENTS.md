# Research artifact guide

Files in this directory are persistent research outputs, not scratchpads.

## Required qualities

- Persist the complete research needed to understand the decision: ranked candidates, direct URLs, rationale, rejected alternatives, final canonical set, and meaningful uncertainty.
- Prefer primary and practitioner-authored sources.
- Verify fast-changing technical facts at research time.
- Never invent or repair a citation from memory.
- Use direct durable URLs rather than transient chat citation tokens.
- Distinguish source-backed facts from curator judgments and inferences.
- Preserve valuable prior findings when updating an artifact; make substantive reversals explicit.
- Do not include private/company-confidential information in this public repository.

## Naming

Domain research uses the same numbered basename as its prompt:

`prompts/ai-product-leadership-NN-<domain>.md`

→

`deep-research/ai-product-leadership-NN-<domain>.md`

The Phase 1 capability map is retained as `deep-research/ai-product-leadership-academy.md` for provenance.
