# AI Product Leadership Academy — Domain 09: AI-Native Knowledge Work & Delegation

## Mission

Research **AI-Native Knowledge Work & Delegation only** and identify the smallest canonical set of resources that produces the largest increase in practical capability for an AI-minded product leader.

This prompt is standalone.

## Academy context

Audience: PM through Principal PM and AI-minded builders. Environment: Python-first, Snowflake-centric, Copilot-native enterprise. Favor durable delegation and verification skill over prompt tricks.

Master prompt: `https://github.com/halkypi/AI-Product-Leadership-Academy/blob/main/prompts/ai-product-leadership-00-academy.md`

Phase 1 map: `https://github.com/halkypi/AI-Product-Leadership-Academy/blob/main/deep-research/ai-product-leadership-academy.md`

## Domain boundary

This domain is about becoming an effective **AI-augmented knowledge worker** who can delegate, verify, and collaborate with AI continuously.

Cover:
- task decomposition and delegation
- research and synthesis
- analysis and critique
- writing and editing
- structured reasoning support
- using multiple tools/modes appropriately
- verification and fact-checking
- managing context
- reusable instructions/working conventions
- recognizing the jagged capability frontier
- deciding when AI should not be used
- evolving from one-shot prompting toward longer-running delegated work

Reject prompt libraries, “magic prompts,” persona tricks, and productivity hype without durable evidence.

## Research task

Find and evaluate **no more than 10 candidate resources**. Rank them strongest to weakest and recommend the **smallest canonical set**.

For each candidate include only: Title; Author/organization; Direct URL; Estimated time; Difficulty; Capability gained — one sentence; Decision `CANON` / `REFERENCE` / `DEFER` / `REJECT`; Why — maximum 100 words; Main competitor it beats or why it loses — one sentence.

Evaluate capability/hour, practicality, durability, enterprise relevance, builder mindset, and teaching quality. Prefer rigorous practitioner material and empirical human-AI research.

## Output discipline — curation, not curriculum

This artifact is a **curator decision record**, not a productivity guide. Do not teach delegation techniques, invent prompt frameworks, create daily-work routines, prescribe workflows, or write exercises. Identify the strongest sources that teach durable delegation and verification capability instead.

Required findings structure:
1. **Domain boundary** — 3–6 concise bullets.
2. **Ranked candidates** — up to 10.
3. **Canonical set** — usually 2–5 resources with a brief minimum-sufficient-set rationale.
4. **Comparative findings** — maximum 500 words total, only what is necessary to justify rankings.
5. **Rejected / deferred** — what lost and where it belongs if another domain owns it.
6. **Uncertainties / version notes** — material caveats only.

Aim for **2,000–3,000 words; hard maximum 3,500 words** excluding URLs/table markup. Brevity is a quality criterion.
